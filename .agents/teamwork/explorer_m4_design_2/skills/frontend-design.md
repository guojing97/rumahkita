# Frontend Design Skill Reference
Source: /Users/mac/.gemini/config/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md

Core Methodology:
- Distinct visual identity avoiding generic AI templates / clichés.
- Ground designs in subject matter (RumahKita: Indonesian household finance & pantry inventory).
- Purposeful palette (neo-brutalism tokens: #C8F169 neon lime, #FFFDF7 warm cream, #171717 deep charcoal ink).
- Deliberate typography & clear type scales. Avoid single-word accents and generic tracking.
- Structural devices (borders, shadows) encode information.
- Spend boldness in one place, keep everything else quiet and disciplined.
- Authentic copywriting: clear active verbs, sentence case, no filler copy.
